# NLP/AI Researcher — Linguistic Diversity & Foundation Models

## Overview

We are seeking an NLP/AI researcher to work at the intersection of linguistic diversity, human cognition, and foundation models.

The position is embedded in a research program that treats the world’s languages as a large-scale natural experiment to probe and extend current AI systems. A central goal is to identify how structural variation across languages affects model behavior, generalization, and reasoning—both in humans and machines.

The position is planned for a period of 1 year with a possible extension subject to performance and funding. Ideal start date is during the second half of 2026.

If interested, send a message with the header AI POSITION, your CV and a very brief motivational statement at damian.blasi[at]upf.edu

---

## Research Directions

The successful candidate will contribute to and help shape projects such as:

### Cross-linguistic evaluation of LLMs
- Quantifying how typological and grammatical variation impacts performance across tasks (e.g., reasoning, reference, categorization)

### Cognition–AI interface
- Using cross-linguistic behavioral and descriptive data to probe the limits of current models
